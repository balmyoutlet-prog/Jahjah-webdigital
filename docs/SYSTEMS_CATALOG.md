## 2. New system pages — content / محتوى الصفحات الجديدة

### POS — Point of sale / cashier system (`/pos-system`)
- **Headline:** A cashier system that records every sale, and tells you what sold, at which branch and with which payment method.
- **For:** shops and boutiques, cafés and bakeries, any counter that still runs on a notebook or an old cash register; multi-branch businesses that want one view.
- **Modules:** fast sale screen (tablet / PC / phone, browser-based) · products, variants & barcodes · cash, card & split payments · receipts (print or WhatsApp) · end-of-day cash-up per cashier & branch · discounts & offers with permissions · links to inventory and accounting so each sale updates both.
- **FAQ:** Does it work with my printer, scanner or card terminal? *Hardware is checked during discovery. Most USB/Bluetooth receipt printers and barcode scanners work, but card-terminal integration depends on your bank or provider.* · Does it work offline? *We scope offline mode during discovery if your internet is unreliable.* · Is it a fiscal/tax-certified POS? *Tax and e-invoicing rules differ by country (e.g. ZATCA in Saudi Arabia). We confirm requirements with your accountant before we build. We don't claim certification we don't have.*

### INV — Stock & inventory system (`/inventory-system`)
- **Headline:** Know what's in stock, what's running low and what's being lost, before it costs you.
- **For:** restaurants tracking ingredients and food cost · shops and online stores with many products and sizes · wholesalers and distributors with warehouses · contractors tracking materials per site.
- **Modules:** items, units & variants · stock in / out / transfers between branches or warehouses · low-stock alerts & reorder lists · suppliers & purchase orders · stock counts (جرد) with difference reports · recipes / bill of materials (e.g. a burger uses 150g of meat, so stock goes down automatically per sale) · expiry & batch tracking where needed · stock value reports.
- **FAQ:** Can it connect to my online store or POS? *Yes, when we build both. Connecting to third-party software is assessed during discovery.* · Can I count stock from my phone? *Yes. It runs in the browser, and barcode scanning by camera is scoped where needed.*

### BOOK — Booking & appointments system (`/booking-system`)
- **Headline:** Let customers book online 24/7, and cut no-shows with reminders.
- **For:** salons, spas and barbers · clinics and dental centres · gyms and studios (classes) · hotels and guesthouses (direct room booking) · restaurants (table reservations) · consultants and offices.
- **Modules:** online booking page linked from Google, Instagram and WhatsApp · services, staff and opening hours · calendar per staff member/room/table · deposits or prepayment where a payment provider is connected · reminders (one-tap WhatsApp, or automatic email/SMS where scoped) · customer history & notes · Arabic & English.
- **FAQ:** Can it store medical records? *Patient files carry legal and privacy duties. We scope them separately and only with your compliance requirements written down. A booking system on its own stores appointments, not medical records.* · Can customers pay a deposit? *Yes, once a payment provider is connected.*

### MGMT — Business management system (`/business-management-system`)
- **Headline:** One private dashboard for your team, tasks, branches and numbers, instead of ten WhatsApp groups.
- **For:** businesses with several branches or sites · contractors and maintenance companies running projects · teams where tasks get lost in chats · owners who want daily numbers without calling everyone.
- **Modules:** owner dashboard (sales, expenses, open tasks per branch) · staff accounts & roles · tasks and checklists (opening/closing, cleaning, site checklists) · projects & job tracking for contractors · attendance / shifts / timesheets · approvals (purchases, leave, discounts) · documents & files per client or project · links to accounting, inventory and POS.
- **FAQ:** Is this an ERP? *It's a tailored system with only the modules you need, not a large ERP licence. If you outgrow it, your data can be exported.* · Can it do payroll? *Payroll rules are country-specific. We can track hours and amounts and export them for your accountant; full payroll is scoped separately.*

### SOCIAL — Instagram & LinkedIn management (`/social-media-management`)
- **Headline:** An Instagram and LinkedIn presence that sends people to your website and WhatsApp, not just likes.
- **For:** restaurants, cafés, salons and shops (Instagram first) · B2B companies, contractors, clinics and professional firms (LinkedIn first) · businesses whose profile, bio and links don't match their website and Google listing.
- **Modules:** profile setup or clean-up (bio, highlights, links, LinkedIn company page) · content plan per month (posts, reels, carousels) in Arabic and English · post captions and hashtags written for your business · link-in-bio to menu, ordering, booking or WhatsApp · consistency between Instagram, LinkedIn, Google and the website (same name, phone, services) · simple monthly report (reach, profile visits, link clicks, messages).
- **FAQ:** Do you buy followers or fake engagement? *Never. It breaks platform rules and hurts reach.* · Do you run paid ads on Instagram? *Meta ad campaigns can be scoped separately; ad budget is paid by you directly to Meta.* · Who owns the accounts? *You do. We work as a manager or collaborator.*

### What accounting means for each business / المحاسبة حسب كل بزنس
The accounting system is shaped differently per business. The examples below are confirmed with the client's accountant during discovery:

- **Restaurants & cafés:** daily cash-up per shift, food cost % from recipes, supplier payables, delivery-app vs direct sales split.
- **Bakeries & sweets:** ingredient costs per product, pre-order deposits, waste/expiry write-offs.
- **Retail & online stores:** cost of goods sold, stock valuation, returns & refunds, VAT on sales and purchases where applicable.
- **Salons & spas:** revenue per service and per staff member, staff commissions, product sales vs service sales.
- **Clinics:** invoices per patient visit, insurance claims tracking (amount claimed vs received), consumables cost.
- **Gyms:** membership billing and renewals, revenue recognised per period, class-pack balances.
- **Real estate:** commissions per deal and per agent, rent collection and landlord statements for managed properties.
- **Contractors:** job costing per project (materials, labour, subcontractors), progress billing, retentions.
- **Hotels:** room revenue per night, guest folios, OTA commissions vs direct bookings.
- **Wholesale & distribution:** customer credit limits, receivables aging, sales-rep collections, supplier payables.
- **Professional firms:** time and billing per client or matter, retainers, project profitability.

### Recommended starting bundle per business / الباقة المقترحة للبداية
- **Restaurant:** Website + Digital menu + Direct/WhatsApp ordering + Google Business Profile, then Kitchen display + Inventory (food cost) + Accounting.
- **Retail shop:** POS + Inventory, then Online store + Google Merchant Center + Instagram.
- **Salon / clinic / gym:** Website + Booking system + Google Business Profile, then CRM (reminders, loyalty) + Accounting.
- **Contractor:** Website + LinkedIn, then Business management (projects) + Accounting (job costing) + Inventory (materials).
- **Wholesaler:** Inventory + Accounting (credit & aging), then a B2B ordering portal + CRM for reps.
- **Professional firm:** Website + LinkedIn + CRM, then Business management + Time & billing.

## 4. Honesty rules for the website and for AI assistants
1. Prices: fixed packages use the site's existing constants. Every system above is "quoted after a free discovery call".
2. No `AggregateRating` / review stars in structured data until there are real, verifiable reviews (e.g. from Google Business Profile). Fake ratings in schema break Google's guidelines and can cause a manual penalty.
3. No guarantees of rankings, AI citations, followers or revenue.
4. Integrations (POS hardware, card terminals, delivery apps, e-invoicing such as ZATCA) are "assessed during discovery". They're never claimed upfront.
